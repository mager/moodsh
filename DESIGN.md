# Mood Shell website

## Scene and register

A developer at their desk in afternoon light, trying colors as casually as choosing a notebook. This is a brand surface with a useful theme workbench attached. The physical reference is a fan of paint swatches beside a real terminal, not a dashboard.

## Color

Committed lavender canvas, deep plum ink, vivid violet actions, mint and apricot accents. Neutrals are tinted. Named mood palettes come directly from the Rust implementation and remain exact hex values in previews. Dark terminal backdrops are illustrative and clearly separate from downloadable prompt palettes.

## Typography

Bricolage Grotesque Variable for large, tactile display lettering; Manrope Variable for reading and controls; JetBrains Mono for actual commands. Self-host the fonts. Tight display tracking, generous body leading, restrained small labels. Reject generic Inter, Space Grotesk, and editorial serif pairings for this identity.

## Layout and interaction

Wide asymmetric hero, a large working terminal preview, generous colored sections, simple navigation. Theme tiles are download artifacts, not feature cards. Visible focus rings, real links and buttons, accessible form labels, inline feedback, reduced-motion support. No decorative gradients or scroll effects.
